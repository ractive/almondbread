## What it is

The video catalogue of Maths Town, the self-described largest fractal artwork channel on YouTube, plus archived YouTube descriptions with render notes.

## What it covers

- Depths: "A Trip to Infinity" 1.2e1077 4K60, "Deepish" 4e2656, "The Hardest Zoom" ~7e1299.
- Render times: over a month on a 64-core CPU for "The Hardest Zoom", ~4 months of data plus ~55 h of After Effects export for "The Perpendicular Mandelbrot", ~2 weeks for "The Edge of Infinity" (figures via search summaries, not opened individually).
- "Eye of the Universe" licensed for The Big Bang Theory S12E19 (<https://www.maths.town/videos/eye-of-the-universe-video/>).

## Reliability

Creator's own statements. Render times date from 2017 to 2020 KF + series-approximation pipelines and overstate what current BLA renderers need.
