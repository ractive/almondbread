# Frax help: Explore (precision limit)

**URL:** <https://fr.ax/help/explore> (year approximate)

## What it is

Frax's own help page on exploring, zoom and image sizes.

## What it covers

- On iPhone you can tap-zoom "roughly 30 times… before you reach the 64bit precision limit… about two hundred thousand billion times magnified" (~2e14).
- The "64 bit limit on iOS is absolute and final"; CPU and GPU rendering, image "constantly re-evaluated" during animation.
- Image sizes: 3.1 MP and 12.6 MP for all, 50.3 MP for Pro, 81× supersampling.
