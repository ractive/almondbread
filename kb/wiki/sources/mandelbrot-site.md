## What it is

The author's write-up of mandelbrot.site, a free open-source web explorer.

## What it covers

- Rust compiled to WebAssembly in Web Workers, Leaflet map-style tiles, perturbation with one high-precision reference, "unlimited zoom".
- Share links that reopen the same location and settings; PNG with embedded parameters; raw iteration data download; zoom animations recorded as video.

## Reliability

Primary description by the author. It shows that links and video export are available free in a browser, so neither is unique on its own.
