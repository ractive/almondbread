# Wikipedia: Mandelbulb

**URL:** <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandelbulb> · read 2026-10-19, not saved

## What it covers

- Developed 2009 by Daniel White and Paul Nylander using spherical coordinates; the
  "n-th power" of a 3D vector multiplies the two angles by n and raises the radius to n;
  iterate `v -> v^n + c` from the origin. "Many of their graphic renderings use n = 8."
- For odd n the power reduces to rational polynomials; (p, q) and other variants exist.
- Lead image is "ray-marched"; links Mandelbulber. Nothing on precision or zoom.
