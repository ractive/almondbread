# NIST CODATA 2022 recommended values

**URL:** <https://physics.nist.gov/cuu/Constants/> (one page per constant, read
2026-10-15). NIST currently serves the **2022** CODATA adjustment. Not saved under `raw/`.

**h c in eV m:** 1.239 841 984e-6 eV m, computed from the exact h (eV) and c above
(2026-10-15). The guessed NIST page `Value?hcev` returns "ill-formed request", so this
product is derived, not quoted; both factors are exact, so the product is too (to the
digits shown). Use it to convert a photon energy to a wavelength (λ = hc / E), e.g.
13.6 TeV ↔ 9.1e-20 m.

## Reliability

Highest available: CODATA is the internationally adopted set. h and c are exact by the
2019 SI definitions. Planck length and time are derived from G and carry its 1e-5
relative uncertainty; they are scale markers, not physically probed lengths.
