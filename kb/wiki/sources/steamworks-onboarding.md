# Steamworks: Onboarding and app fee

**URL:** <https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/gettingstarted/onboarding> · <https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/gettingstarted/appfee> (fetched 2026-10-14)

## What it is

Valve's pages on joining Steamworks and releasing a first app.

## What it covers

- Sign NDA and distribution agreement, pay a $100 fee per app (recouped at $1,000 adjusted gross revenue), give bank, tax (W-9 / W-8BEN) and identity data; verification "may take 10 to 15 business days".
- A 21-day wait between paying the fee and release (another summary said 30 days).
- Coming Soon page public for at least two weeks; store page and build reviews take 1–5 days each.

## Reliability

Primary, but the waiting period conflicts between the fetched page and a search summary; re-check before planning.
