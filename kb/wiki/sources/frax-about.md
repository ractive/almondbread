# Frax: What is Frax?

**URL:** <https://www.fr.ax/about> · read 2026-10-19, not saved (year approximate)

## What it covers

- iOS fractal app by Ben Weiss and Kai Krause (with Tom Beddard, per a mirrored blog post,
  unverified): classic Mandelbrot, Julia sets that "can be zoomed… infinitely", "20 of them in
  four sets" of formulas.
- Raises the fractal in "3D height", two light sources with glossy reflections, "infinitely
  scaleable chaotic textures", almost 100 parameters; colours and lights animate in real time;
  "400 times faster" than the original by a CPU/GPU design.
- Mandelbrot depth "more than a trillion-to-one" (the 64-bit limit, see
  [Frax help: Explore (precision limit)](frax-help-explore.md)). A third-party listing calls development dormant (unverified).
