## What it is

PLOS ONE paper deriving cividis from viridis with simulated deuteranopia: it looks the same to normal and red-green colour-blind viewers (blue to yellow).

## What it covers

- Built with the Machado CVD model; lightness made linear.
- The CVD-safe reference palette.
