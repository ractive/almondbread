# Wikipedia: Skewes's number

**URL:** <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skewes%27s_number> · **Saved:** not saved (`raw: "web"`) · **Terms:** CC BY-SA

## What it is

Encyclopedia article on Skewes's upper bounds for the first sign change of `π(x) − li(x)`. Read
through a summarising fetch on 2026-10-15.

## What it covers

- Skewes 1933, assuming the Riemann hypothesis: a crossing below `e^e^e^79 < 10^10^10^34`.
- Skewes 1955, without it: below `e^e^e^e^7.705 < 10^10^10^964`.
- Both are upper bounds, not the true first crossing (which is far smaller).
