# Make: Arthur Ganson's "Machine with Concrete"

**URL:** <https://makezine.com/article/craft/arthur-gansons-machine-with-concrete/> · **Saved:** not saved (`raw: "web"`) · **Terms:** publisher copyright; facts only

## What it is

Short Make magazine article (2012-04-25, updated 2016-11-10) on Arthur Ganson's kinetic sculpture.
Read through a summarising fetch on 2026-10-15. Wikipedia's Arthur Ganson page only says the last
gear turns once in "over 2 trillion years" and is embedded in concrete.

## What it covers

- "A gear train consisting of twelve pairs of worms and gears", each reducing the speed by 1/50.
- "The input shaft is constantly driven at 200 rpm."
- "It will take well over two trillion years before the final gear makes but one turn."
- "The final gear is embedded in a block of solid concrete."

## Reliability

Consistent with Wikipedia and with our arithmetic: `50^12 / 200 rpm` = 1.22·10^18 min =
2.32·10^12 years. Some posts (Boing Boing 2009) say 212 rpm; not used.
