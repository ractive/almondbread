# Apple EU App Store terms, October 2026 (MacRumors)

**URL:** <https://macrumors.com/2026/08/18/eu-app-store-fee-change> · also <https://www.iclarified.com/101814/apple-revises-eu-app-store-terms-replaces-core-technology-fee-with-5-commission>

## What it is

News coverage (2026-08-18) of Apple's revised EU terms, effective 2026-10-01.

## What it covers

- App Store IAP 26% standard, 15% for Small Business Program and similar programs.
- Alternative payment in-app 20% (10% for program members); link-out 15% (10% for members).
- Alternative marketplaces and web distribution pay a 5% Core Technology Commission; the per-install Core Technology Fee is removed.

## Reliability

Secondary press coverage of Apple's announcement; verify against Apple's developer pages before acting. Applies to iOS/iPadOS in the EU, not to direct Mac sales.
