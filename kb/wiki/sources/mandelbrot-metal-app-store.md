# Mandelbrot Metal (App Store listing)

**URL:** <https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mandelbrot-metal/id6749807290> · lookup <https://itunes.apple.com/lookup?id=6749807290&country=us> (fetched 2026-10-14)

## What it is

A universal (iPhone, iPad, Mac M1+, Vision; OS 26) Mandelbrot explorer first released 2025-10-13, with about 25 versions between February and October 2026. v3.0 (2026-09-22) added GPU perturbation and switched to freemium with a subscription: Pro $2.99/month, $19.99/year, $39.99 lifetime; earlier buyers get "Legacy Pro". US 4.79 stars from 28 ratings.

## What it covers

- "GPU perturbation rendering… Adaptive precision, responsive gesture previews, and center-out refinement" (progressive).
- 156 palettes, 50+ bundled bookmarks, Julia for any point, 16 languages; Pro adds custom palettes, 3D lighting, 4K to 8K PNG.

## Reliability

Developer self-description. The only deep-zoom fractal app found with a subscription, so it is the pricing experiment to watch.
