## What it is

Four posts by the author of KF2+, Fraktaler 3 and the `mandelbrot-numerics` /
`mandelbrot-symbolics` / `mandelbrot-perturbator` libraries, on making Julia-morphing
zoom sequences by program instead of by hand.

## What it covers

- **2016, by external angles.** A morph sequence of periods 5, 69, 143, 291 (each `2p + 5`)
  is generated by extrapolating the binary external angles block-wise (new angle = old
  blocks concatenated plus a seed block). Each step traces the ray inwards (`2 · period · 8`
  steps), refines the nucleus by Newton (up to 64 steps) and frames the view at 12× the
  distance between the last two nuclei. Cost: ray tracing is about `O(p²)`, so each level
  costs more than 4× the last; the last location took over 24 hours, "dwarfing" the render
  time: "works in theory, but it's not practical computationally".
- **2016, symmetry.** The automatic method could pick rotation 1/3 or 2/3 unpredictably; 16
  renders of all choices show the central morphed tree "almost indistinguishable", only its
  alignment with the surrounding ring differs.
- **2017, O(p) method** (`m-ejs-tree-morph.c` in mandelbrot-numerics). Insights:
  (1) the primary spirals and tips of an embedded Julia set are told apart by the
  **preperiod** of the Misiurewicz point at their centre; Newton for Misiurewicz points
  (dividing out lower preperiods) has basins that "comfortably enclose the center of the
  embedded Julia set"; (2) from a Misiurewicz centre, Newton finds nuclei along a spiral spine
  whose periods grow by multiples of the influencing island's period, and from a nucleus
  Newton finds the next Misiurewicz point; (3) the **atom-domain coordinate** of the next
  treeward tip is the previous one scaled by a power of 1.5 (only in the limit), which gives
  the Newton start. Pitfall: a **symmetry trap**, basin boundaries pass through the start
  point, so Newton fails when started exactly on a symmetry centre. The period "typically
  more than doubles at each next level of morphing". Inputs: an angled internal address,
  influencing-island period, embedded-Julia period, rotation denominator, "arm length" (main
  artistic knob) and a view-size multiplier set by hand.
- **2021, a morph orbit.** A video moving around a circuit of Julia morphs near period-7
  islands: prefixes generated from the angle blocks, the suffix changes with the child bulb
  (`2p + k`). Pipeline: `m-exray-in` (ray), `m-nucleus`, `m-domain-size` (zoom level from the
  atom-domain size), a custom aligner, KF for rendering, all in a shell pipeline. Generating
  4,096 rays at depth 12 took almost 2 days.

## Reliability

Primary, by the person who implemented Newton zooming in KF. Heuristics (1.5 power, arm
length, view multiplier) are stated as empirical and limited by the author.
