# Frax (App Store listing and reviews)

**URL:** <https://apps.apple.com/us/app/frax/id568827824> · lookup <https://itunes.apple.com/lookup?id=568827824&country=us> · reviews RSS <https://itunes.apple.com/us/rss/customerreviews/page=1/id=568827824/sortby=mostrecent/json> (fetched 2026-10-14)

## What it is

The best-known iOS fractal app (since 2013-10-02). iPhone, iPad, Vision, and Mac through the iPad app. Free with Pro $4.99 and cloud render credit packs ($0.99 to $9.99). US 4.50 stars from 232 ratings. v2.1.0 (2026-07-11) came after a 20-month gap.

## What it covers

- "A depth range of a trillion-to-one" (double precision); tilt-to-steer flight; Pro unlocks ~100 parameters; 1080p video for Pro; preset links.
- Reviews: repeated complaints about the depth limit (US, GB, DE, 2021 to 2024), pixelated or crashing video recording, render-credit purchase failures, a tedious tutorial, and a gyroscope that cannot be disabled (US 2026-07-15). Praise for relaxation and classroom use.

## Reliability

Store metadata is primary; the review RSS covers only the ~50 most recent written reviews per storefront.
