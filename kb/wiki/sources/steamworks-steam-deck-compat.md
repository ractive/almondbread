# Steamworks: Steam Deck compatibility review

**URL:** <https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/steamdeck/compat> (fetched 2026-10-14)

## What it is

Valve's criteria and process for Deck Verified / Playable / Unsupported ratings.

## What it covers

- Input: default controller config, glyphs matching the device, on-screen keyboard for text.
- Display: 1280×800 or 1280×720; smallest text at least 9 px at 1280×800 (12 px recommended).
- Performance: 30 fps at 800p by default; no unsupported-OS/GPU warnings; launchers discouraged.
- Valve tests the native Linux build first, falls back to Proton, publishes the better result; re-review on request.
