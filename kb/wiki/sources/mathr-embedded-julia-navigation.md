# mathr blog: navigating embedded Julia sets in the hairs

**URLs** (read 2026-10-17, not saved; facts only):
"Navigating in the hairs" (2014-11-18)
<https://mathr.co.uk/blog/2014-11-18_navigating_in_the_hairs.html> and "Patterns in
embedded Julia sets" (2017-11-06)
<https://mathr.co.uk/blog/2017-11-06_patterns_in_embedded_julia_sets.html>.

## What it covers

- Islands in an island's hairs have external angles built from repeated blocks of the island
  and of its influencing island (period 3 in the example: `.(011)`, `.(100)`); appending
  short blocks gives periods 69, 70, 72, 76 from 65. "These patterns are essentially
  counting in binary, with the bits mapped to segments of different lengths."
- Around period-88 islands in the hairs of the period-3 island's 1/5 and 2/5 child bulbs
  (period 15), embedded Julia sets show 5-armed spirals whose periods rise by 3 per step
  around (1/5) or per two steps (2/5). The rays split the set in halves recursively: "1
  island of period 88, 2 of 91, 4 of 94, 8 of 97, and so on" (conjectured general, except
  perhaps near the cusp and antenna tips).
- An island's seahorse valley hairs hold embedded Julia sets like those near −0.75 in the
  main set.

## Reliability

Primary; the binary subdivision is the author's conjecture from observation.
