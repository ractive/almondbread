# Wikipedia: Kruskal's tree theorem (TREE(3))

**URL:** <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kruskal%27s_tree_theorem> · **Saved:** not saved (`raw: "web"`) · **Terms:** CC BY-SA

## What it is

Encyclopedia article on Kruskal's tree theorem and Friedman's TREE function. Read through a
summarising fetch on 2026-10-15.

## What it covers

- "An *extremely* weak lower bound for TREE(3)" is `A^A(187196)(1)`, with `A` the one-argument
  Ackermann function.
- TREE(3) exceeds many other "large" combinatorial constants, among them Friedman's n(4) and
  Graham's number.
- The TREE function "grows far faster than `f_Γ₀(n)`" in the fast-growing hierarchy; Friedman
  computed an ordinal with a similar growth rate, which the page does not state.
- Friedman: TREE(3) exceeds the halting time of any Turing machine provably halting in
  ACA₀ + Π¹₂-BI with at most `2↑↑1000` symbols (FOM post 273, 2006; "Enormous Integers in Real
  Life", 2000).

## Reliability

The bounds are Friedman's, reported second-hand. Fine for the one-liner in the panel; the
`f_θ(Ω^ω ω)` growth rate often quoted elsewhere is not on this page, so we do not use it.
