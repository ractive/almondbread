# KF Movie Maker user guide (Maths Town)

**URL:** <https://www.maths.town/after-effects-plugins/kfmoviemaker/kfmoviemaker-user-guide/> (year approximate)

## What it is

Guide to KF Movie Maker, a free AGPL After Effects plug-in that turns Kalles Fraktaler `.kfb` iteration files plus a `.kfr` colour file into a continuous zoom video.

## What it covers

- Requires paid Adobe After Effects ("you will need to buy it, or setup a subscription").
- Zoom size must be exactly 2; keyframes animated by hand (~1.5 s each); render through Adobe Media Encoder.
- Pain points: KF export can take "hours, days, weeks or months"; 4K/8K slow; colour animation invalidates caches; crashes with layer sampling; YouTube bitrate too low for fast zooms.

## Reliability

Primary documentation of the leading creator's own pipeline; the best evidence of the creator workflow almondbread could replace.
