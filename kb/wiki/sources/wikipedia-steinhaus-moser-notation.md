# Wikipedia: Steinhaus–Moser notation (mega, Moser's number)

**URL:** <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steinhaus%E2%80%93Moser_notation> · **Saved:** not saved (`raw: "web"`) · **Terms:** CC BY-SA

## What it is

Encyclopedia article on the polygon notation for large numbers. Read through a summarising fetch
on 2026-10-15.

## What it covers

- Mega (2 in a circle): `10↑↑257 < mega < 10↑↑258`; cruder, `mega ≈ 256↑↑257`.
- Moser's number: `moser < 3→3→4→2` (Conway chains) and `moser < f³(4)` with `f(n) = 3↑ⁿ3`.
- "Vanishingly small compared to Graham's number": `moser ≪ 3→3→64→2 < f⁶⁴(4) = G`.

## Reliability

Only upper bounds for Moser's number are given; no lower bound in arrow terms. That is why the
panel places mega but not Moser's number. Note `3→3→4→2 = 3 ↑^(3→3→4) 3 = 3 ↑^(g₁) 3 = g₂`
(our own reading of Conway chains, (unverified) against a source).
