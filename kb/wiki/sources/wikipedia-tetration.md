## What it is

Encyclopedia article on tetration (iterated exponentiation) and its extensions to real heights.
Read through a summarising fetch on 2026-10-15; only the first ~100 000 characters were covered,
so the super-logarithm section itself was not read.

## What it covers

- §Linear approximation for real heights: `ˣa = log_a(^(x+1)a)` for `x ≤ -1`, `1 + x` for
  `-1 < x ≤ 0`, `a^(^(x-1)a)` for `x > 0`.
- The function is continuous; in general only piecewise differentiable (the derivative jumps by a
  factor `ln a` at the integers); continuously differentiable for `x > -2` only for base `e`.
- Attributes the uniqueness theorem for this extension to M. H. Hooshmand (2006), whose name
  for it is "ultra exponential". Whether Hooshmand originated the approximation is not stated.
