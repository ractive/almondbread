# RevenueCat State of Subscription Apps 2026

**URL:** <https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/subscription-app-trends-benchmarks-2026> · report <https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps>

## What it is

Annual benchmark report over 115k+ subscription apps and $16B+ revenue.

## What it covers

- Median day-35 conversion: hard paywall 10.7% vs freemium 2.1%; revenue per install at day 60: $3.09 vs $0.38.
- Trial-to-paid 42.5% for 17 to 32 day trials vs 25.5% under 4 days.
- About 72% of annual subscribers cancel in year one; 400× revenue gap between top 5% and bottom 25% of apps.

## Reliability

Large vendor dataset, but subscription apps only; it does not measure one-time unlocks. Category cuts are behind a gated download.
