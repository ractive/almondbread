# Crameri: Scientific colour maps

**URL:** <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.1243862> · **Saved:** not saved; notes in `raw/research/fractal-palettes/perceptual-maps.md` · **Terms:** MIT (Zenodo record metadata; cmcrameri package)

## What it is

Perceptually uniform, colour-vision-deficiency-friendly maps (version 8.0.1): sequential (batlow, hawaii, lajolla, oslo, tokyo, davos, imola, ...), diverging, multi-sequential and cyclic (romaO, vikO, bamO, brocO, corkO).

## What it covers

- batlow is the flagship: perceptually uniform, CVD-safe and readable in greyscale.
- Data: 256 lines of `R G B` floats per map (cmcrameri `cmaps/<name>.txt`).
- Crameri et al. 2020 (Nature Communications, CC BY 4.0) argue against rainbow and red-green maps.
