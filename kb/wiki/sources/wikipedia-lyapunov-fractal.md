# Wikipedia: Lyapunov fractal

**URL:** <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lyapunov_fractal> · read 2026-10-19, not saved

## What it covers

- Logistic map `x_n = r_n x_{n-1}(1 - x_{n-1})` with `r_n` taken from a periodic sequence of
  two values A, B (e.g. "AB"), plotted in the (a, b) plane; `x_0 = 0.5` (the critical point).
- Colour by the Lyapunov exponent `λ ≈ (1/N) Σ log|r_n (1 - 2 x_n)|`: stable (`λ < 0`) vs chaos.
- Algorithm credited to Mario Markus (1990). No cost figures.
