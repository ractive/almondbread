# Fractal Dive (App Store listing)

**URL:** <https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fractal-dive/id6813485724> · metadata via <https://itunes.apple.com/lookup?id=6813485724&country=us> (fetched 2026-10-14)

## What it is

The store listing of Fractal Dive (newlin.nl), a universal iPhone, iPad, Mac and Vision deep-zoom explorer. v1.0 shipped 2026-09-20, v1.1 (2026-10-03) made the app free, v1.2 shipped 2026-10-12. Pro is a one-time $1.99 IAP with Family Sharing. No ratings yet.

## What it covers

- Perturbation: one arbitrary-precision orbit on the CPU, differences on the GPU; "tested to four times ten to the power 331"; z², z³ and a mirrored variant.
- "Dive" autopilot that seeks the richest region; free exact share links ("lands exactly there, same colours").
- Pro: 4K/8K stills, dive video re-rendered at 1080p/4K, 9 colour schemes (3 free), iteration/distance/orbit-trap colouring.
- Depth analogies ("smaller than a cell, smaller than an atom"); keyboard, scroll and trackpad on Mac.
- Copy: "Nearly every fractal app stalls around a thousand trillion times magnification."

## Reliability

Developer self-description; no independent benchmark. The closest functional analogue to almondbread's plan.
