# cmocean: colour maps for oceanography

**URL:** <https://github.com/matplotlib/cmocean> · **Saved:** not saved; notes in `raw/research/fractal-palettes/perceptual-maps.md` · **Terms:** MIT

## What it is

Perceptually uniform maps named by quantity (thermal, haline, solar, ice, deep, dense, algae, matter, tempo, phase, ...); phase is cyclic.

## What it covers

- Data: 256 lines of float `R G B` per map in `cmocean/rgb/<name>-rgb.txt`.

## Reliability

Primary data source.
