# Wikipedia: Fast-growing hierarchy

**URL:** <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fast-growing_hierarchy> · **Saved:** not saved (`raw: "web"`) · **Terms:** CC BY-SA

## What it is

Encyclopedia article on the ordinal-indexed hierarchy of fast-growing functions `f_α`. Read
through a summarising fetch on 2026-10-15.

## What it covers

- `f_ω(n) = f_n(n) > 2[n+1]n > 2[n](n+3) − 3 = A(n, n)` for `n ≥ 4` (hyperoperations in
  brackets); `f_ω` is a unary version of the Ackermann function. So a fixed number of up-arrows
  sits below level ω, and a variable number of arrows is level ω.
- `f_(ω+1)(64) = f_ω^64(64) > G` (Graham's number).
