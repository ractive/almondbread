# Wikipedia: Fractal flame

**URL:** <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fractal_flame> · read 2026-10-19, not saved

## What it covers

- IFS fractals by Scott Draves (1992). Each step picks one of several weighted functions (an
  affine map followed by a weighted sum of nonlinear "variations"), applies it, and blends the
  point's colour with that function's colour.
- Histogram of visits per cell; brightness by log density with gamma; quality "directly
  proportional to the number of iterations"; supersampled histograms; FLAM3 uses adaptive
  density estimation (filter width inverse to sample count).
- Software: Electric Sheep (distributed screensaver), Apophysis, GIMP.
