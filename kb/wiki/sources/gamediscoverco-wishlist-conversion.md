# GameDiscoverCo: state of Steam, wishlist conversion (Nov 2025)

**URL:** <https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/gamediscoverco-the-state-of-steam>

## What it is

GameDevReports' summary of a GameDiscoverCo talk (3 Nov 2025) on Steam launch data.

## What it covers

- Median first-week sales 0.15× wishlists for launches over 25k wishlists; 0.10× for games above $10.
- No long-term decline in conversion; collecting wishlists is getting harder; conversion varies by an order of magnitude.
- Underperformers: 67% median 7-day user score vs 91% for overperformers; longer pre-release (411 vs 214 days).

## Reliability

Secondary summary of an industry analyst; the dataset skews to games with many wishlists.
