# Wikipedia: Newton fractal

**URL:** <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newton_fractal> · read 2026-10-19, not saved

## What it covers

- The Julia set of the Newton map `z -> z - p(z)/p'(z)`; the plane splits into basins of the
  roots; some polynomials (e.g. `z^3 - 2z + 2`) have open sets trapped in cycles.
- Relaxed Newton `z - a p/p'`; stable for `a` in the disc of radius 1 around 1.
- Nova (Paul Derbyshire, 1990s): add a constant `c` per step; Julia variant fixes `c`,
  Mandelbrot variant takes `c` from the pixel and starts from a critical point.
- Colouring by root reached and by iterations to converge; non-converging points black.
