# HPDZ.NET: Burning Ship and Nova deep zooms

**URLs:** <https://www.hpdz.net/Animations/BurningShip1.htm>, <https://hpdz.net/StillImages/Nova.htm>
· known from search summaries on 2026-10-19, not read in full. Author name as commonly
attributed for HPDZ (unverified).

## What it covers

- A 12 000-frame video described as the first deep-zoom animation of the Burning Ship, to a
  scale of about 2.35e-100; a cubic Burning Ship zoom to 1e-23 that took about two days for 96
  key images.
- Nova still images: at 4.3e-21, 128-bit ("precision 4") arithmetic shows artefactual fuzz,
  removed by 192 bits. Brute-force arbitrary precision, no perturbation.

## Reliability

Primary for its own renders; pre-perturbation era (2010–2011).
