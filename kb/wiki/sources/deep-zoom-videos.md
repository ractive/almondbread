## Reliability

Self-reported. Depth figures are consistent within each description except "Deepish". The
"Seryozha" attribution of "Eye of the Universe" in our brief is wrong: the video is Maths
Town's (SeryZone is a separate artist and software author credited in Dinkydau's videos).
