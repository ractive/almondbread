# How To Market A Game: benchmarks

**URL:** <https://howtomarketagame.com/benchmarks/>

## What it is

A running page of indie Steam marketing benchmarks from surveys and studies.

## What it covers

- Wishlists at launch by tier (updated June 2026): Bronze 5,000, Silver 8,000, Gold 50,000, Diamond 90,000; Bronze is about $1k first-week revenue.
- Weekly wishlists: Bronze 0–40, Silver 15–120, Gold 100–700; Coming Soon day-one wishlists (Sept 2026).
- First-week conversion by wishlist band (15–25%); Next Fest wishlist tiers; demo playtime; Boxleiter ~31 reviews-to-sales (old data).

## Reliability

Survey-based and self-selected; some study dates conflict (2020 vs 2025). Good for go/no-go bands, not forecasts.
