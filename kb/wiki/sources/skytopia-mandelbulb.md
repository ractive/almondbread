# Daniel White: Mandelbulb (skytopia)

**URL:** <https://www.skytopia.com/project/fractal/mandelbulb.html> · read 2026-10-19, not saved

## What it is

The co-discoverer's own account of how the Mandelbulb was found (with Paul Nylander, 2009).

## What it covers

- White's 2007 spherical-rotation formula looked "nice, but not exactly what I'd call a 3D
  Mandelbrot": zooms showed no true 3D detail. Quaternion sets are "'whipped cream' swirls".
- Nylander's power-8 version showed "buds growing on buds".
- David Makin's zooms, the first beyond 1000×, showed detail "in all three dimensions"; deep
  views showed "no sign of detail degradation" (at the depths of 2009, i.e. small).
- White's own verdict: the 2D set remains more "organic and rich"; no 3D candidate has "the
  variety of the Mandelbrot after zooming in".

## Reliability

Primary but promotional and from 2009; no numbers on depth.
