## Why it doubles

Close to a minibrot, one round of its loop acts like a fresh "square and add". **Squaring doubles every angle**, so whatever shape lies around the spot gets wrapped around twice: 3 arms become 6, 6 become 12, and so on.

The rings you passed are these copies, one inside the other. People call such a ring an *embedded Julia set*.

## What deep-zoom videos show
place: embedded-julia

Here near the Airplane on the spike, a tiny minibrot (too small to see at this zoom) wears a ruff of spirals shaped like a Julia set.

Deep-zoom videos dive towards minibrots thousands of digits deep and pass dozens of these doublings. Artists steer a little off centre on the way, to choose which shape gets doubled next: that is called *Julia morphing*.
