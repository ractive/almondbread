## Bulbs on the heart
center: -0.45, 0
span: 2.6
look: aurora
interior: period
orbit: on
orbit-length: 200
probe: -0.2, 0
marker: off

Around the big heart (the main cardioid) sit bulbs of every size. Inside the heart the numbers settle on **one** point (the ringed c = −0.2 here): period 1.

Inside a bulb they settle into a loop of several points, the same loop length for the whole bulb: its **period**. The period colouring gives every period its own colour.

Between the bulbs c passes through the heart (1 point) or briefly outside the set, where the dots fly off.

## Fractions
center: -0.25, 0.4
span: 1.3
probe: -0.12256116687665362, 0.74486176661974424
marker: off

Go round the heart from the dent at its right. The disc on the left sits **halfway** round: period 2. The big top bulb sits a **third** of the way round: period 3. The period-4 bulb a **quarter**, the period-5 bulb a **fifth**.

Every bulb has a fraction like that, and the **bottom number is its period**. Some fractions have a 2 or 3 on top: the period-5 bulb on the upper left sits **two fifths** of the way round.

## Between two bulbs
center: -0.36, 0.6
span: 0.45
probe: -0.504340175446244, 0.56276576145298196
marker: -0.504340175446244, 0.56276576145298196
marker-label: 2/5: period 5

Which is the biggest bulb between the 1/2 disc and the 1/3 bulb? Add the tops and add the bottoms: (1 + 1) / (2 + 3) = **2/5**. So its period is 2 + 3 = **5**: the ringed bulb.

## And again
probe: -0.35910239011244916, 0.61735345339882686
marker: -0.35910239011244916, 0.61735345339882686
marker-label: 3/8: period 8

It goes on for ever. Between 2/5 and 1/3: (2 + 1) / (5 + 3) = **3/8**, period 8, the ringed bulb. Between 1/2 and 2/5: 3/7, period 7, on its left.

The bigger a bulb's period, the smaller it is: the bulbs on the heart are the fractions, in order and every one of them.
