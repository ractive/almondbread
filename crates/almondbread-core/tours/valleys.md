## Seahorse valley
place: seahorse-valley
interior: flat
orbit: off
map: off
marker: off

The big heart touches the round bulb on its left at one point, the **neck**. The gap just above it is **seahorse valley**. Both walls are lined with curled shapes that people call seahorses.

They are made of tiny **bulbs**, round blobs like the big one, on long chains, and every one of them ends in a **spiral**.

## A seahorse
place: seahorse

One seahorse, close up: a head of bulbs, a curled body, and a tail that winds into a whirl. Its neighbours up and down the valley are the same shape, a little smaller or larger each time.

## The tail
place: seahorse-tail

The tail spirals in, turn after turn, towards one point in its middle: a **Misiurewicz point** (after the mathematician Michał Misiurewicz).

Every point gets the same test: start at 0, square the number, add the point, repeat. For most points the numbers fly off or settle down. Here they do neither: after some steps they land **exactly** on a **loop**, a few numbers repeating for ever, but any number next to the loop gets pushed away from it.

## A double spiral
place: two-arm-spiral
marker: -0.775683768009053797469483503934741045728, 0.136467368294690124733274409617848765198
marker-label: M(24,2)

Another spiral, elsewhere in the valley, with its centre marked: another Misiurewicz point. Its numbers land after **24 steps** on a loop of **2** numbers, so it is called **M(24,2)**.

Its two spiral arms wind into the centre, each turn a little smaller than the last.

## The same, 10⁵⁰ times deeper
place: spiral-1e50
marker: off

This is the same point, magnified **10⁵⁰** times (a 1 with 50 zeros) instead of about 30 000. And it looks the same: two arms, still turning.

Near a Misiurewicz point the set copies itself: zoom in by a certain amount, turn by a certain angle, and you are back at the same picture. That is why spirals never end, however deep you go.

## Elephant valley
place: elephant-valley
move: jump

On the right of the heart is its dent, the **cusp**. The gap it opens is **elephant valley**: its walls are a parade of elephants, their trunks curling inward.

## The herd
place: elephant-herd

Elephants in a row, each smaller than the last. Every elephant is the decoration of a small bulb on the heart near the cusp, and every trunk curls into a spiral around a Misiurewicz point.

## Branch points
place: three-way-junction

Not every Misiurewicz point is a spiral. This one is a **fork**: three branches of the **antenna**, the thin hair on the big top bulb, meet here. After 4 steps its numbers land on a single number and stay there.

## The same fork, a googol deeper
place: junction-1e100

The same point, magnified **10¹⁰⁰** times (a *googol*: a 1 with 100 zeros): still a fork with three branches, now covered in many-armed stars. Spirals and forks: the set is built around these points at every depth.
