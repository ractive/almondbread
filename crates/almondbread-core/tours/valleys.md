## Seahorse valley
place: seahorse-valley
interior: flat
orbit: off
map: off
marker: off

The gap between the big heart and the round disc on its left, just above the neck at −0.75: **seahorse valley**. Both walls are lined with curled shapes that people call seahorses.

They are made of tiny bulbs on long chains, and every one of them ends in a **spiral**.

## A seahorse
place: seahorse

One seahorse, close up: a head of bulbs, a curled body, and a tail that winds into a whirl. Its neighbours up and down the valley are the same shape, a little smaller or larger each time.

## The tail
place: seahorse-tail

The tail spirals in, turn after turn, towards one point in its middle. That point is a **Misiurewicz point** (after the mathematician Michał Misiurewicz).

There, the rule's numbers do something special: they neither fly off nor settle down. After some steps they land **exactly** on a loop and go round it for ever, but the loop pushes every nearby start away.

## A double spiral
place: two-arm-spiral
marker: -0.775683768009053797469483503934741045728, 0.136467368294690124733274409617848765198
marker-label: M(24,2)

Another spiral, elsewhere in the valley: the ring marks its centre. From this c, the numbers land after **24 steps** on a loop of **2** numbers: the point is called **M(24,2)**.

Its two spiral arms wind into the centre, each turn a little smaller than the last.

## The same, 10⁵⁰ times deeper
place: spiral-1e50
marker: off

This is the same point, magnified **10⁵⁰** times instead of about 30 000. And it looks the same: two arms, still turning.

Near a Misiurewicz point the set copies itself: zoom in by a fixed factor, turn by a fixed angle, and you are back at the same picture. That is why spirals never end, however deep you go.

## Elephant valley
place: elephant-valley
move: jump

On the right of the heart is the dent at **0.25**, the cusp. The gap it opens is **elephant valley**: its walls are a parade of elephants, their trunks curling inward.

## The herd
place: elephant-herd

Elephants in a row, each smaller than the last. Every elephant is the decoration of a small bulb on the heart near the cusp, and every trunk curls into a spiral around a Misiurewicz point.

## Branch points
place: three-way-junction

Not every Misiurewicz point is a spiral. This one is a **fork**: three branches of the antenna of the big top bulb meet here, and after 4 steps its numbers land on a single point and stay there.

## The same fork, a googol deeper
place: junction-1e100

The same point, **10¹⁰⁰** times magnified: still a fork with three branches, now covered in many-armed stars. Spirals and forks: the set is built around these points at every depth.
