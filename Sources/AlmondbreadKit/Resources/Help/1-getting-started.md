# Getting started & controls

Almondbread is a window into the Mandelbrot set, a shape made by one tiny rule repeated over and over. You can zoom into it almost forever: every edge hides more detail, spirals and small copies of the whole shape.

Start anywhere. Point at something interesting and zoom in. If you get lost, press **R** to go back to the whole set.

## On a Mac

- **Move the mouse** to choose where to zoom. The pointer becomes a small crosshair over the picture.
- **Hold Space** to zoom in, **Shift + Space** to zoom out.
- **Pinch** on the trackpad, scroll with **⌘** held, or turn the mouse wheel to zoom.
- **Drag**, or scroll with two fingers, to move the picture.
- **Double-click** zooms in there; **⌥ double-click** zooms out.
- **W A S D** or the **arrow keys** move the picture; zooming then goes to the middle.
- **E** and **Q** are pedals: **E** starts a steady zoom in (cruise), **Q** a zoom out, each press a step faster; press the other one to slow down. Hold Space while pressing them to change the Space zoom speed instead.

## On iPhone and iPad

- **Drag** to move, **pinch** to zoom.
- **Double tap** zooms in; a **two-finger tap** zooms out.
- **Twist** two fingers to turn the picture.
- The round buttons start the autopilot or a journey and open the control center.
- With a keyboard attached, the Mac keys work too.

## Keys

- **F**: full screen on or off
- **E** / **Q**: cruise in / out (see *Driving levels*)
- **Z**: autopilot (flies on by itself)
- **L**: lane assist while cruising
- **J**: journey (a planned trip from landmark to landmark); **Shift + J** in the other mode (continuous, or stop at each find)
- **Esc**: stop the cruise, the autopilot, a flight or a journey; then leave full screen
- **C**: cosmic size scale
- **O**: orbit overlay
- **M**: mini-map
- **P**: π meter
- **,** and **.**: turn the picture left or right; **/** turns it back upright
- **+** and **−**: one zoom step
- **R** or **⌘0**: back to the whole set
- **⌘I**: show or hide the control center
- **⌘G**: fly to coordinates; **⇧⌘C** copies where you are
- **⌘?**: this help

## The control center

The control center (**⌘I**, or the slider button) holds every setting: places to visit, colours, what the inside looks like, overlays and quality. Type in its search field to find a setting quickly. The small **?** buttons explain a section, and the **↺** buttons reset it.
