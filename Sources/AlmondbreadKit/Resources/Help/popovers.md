# Popovers

The short texts behind the (?) buttons. One section per topic; the heading is the topic's id.

## cosmic-scale

Your window stays its real size (30 cm if the screen does not say). As you zoom in, the whole set grows instead: a few kilometres across, as big as Earth, then as big as the observable universe.

Go deeper and the whole Mandelbrot set would be as big as an imaginary universe in which an atom is as big as ours. Deeper still, such imaginary universes nest, level after level.

## pi-meter

The number π hides in the Mandelbrot set. Near −0.75 (the pinch between the two big bulbs) and 0.25 (the cusp at the right) points escape very slowly.

The escape count times the distance from that point gives π, and more digits agree the closer you go. Gold digits already match.

## mini-map

The whole set, plus zoomed-in levels on the way to your view. The gold rectangle or crosshair shows where the next level sits. Click (tap) a map to jump there; ⌥-click (hold) flies there.

Click a map to jump there at your current zoom; ⌥-click flies there. **M** shows or hides the maps.

## orbit

The ring is the point under the pointer (on iPhone and iPad: the middle of the picture, or where you chose *Orbit Here*). The dots are the numbers the rule makes from it, step by step.

On the whole-set map the first steps are joined by a line, and the next 100 000 are fine dots, the newest brightest. Inside the set they settle into a loop; outside they wander off, sometimes only after tens of thousands of steps. The line under the map says which: "escapes after 48 213 steps", "settles into a period-3 cycle" or "still undecided". **O** shows or hides it.

## cockpit

How the zoom is driven, like the levels of a car: **Manual** (the steering wheel: you do everything), **Cruise** (the speedometer: **E** / **Q** set a speed in or out, you steer), **Autopilot** (the plane, **Z**: it steers to interesting places), **Journey** (the sparkles, **J**: from landmark to landmark). The lit icon is the level now; click an icon to switch.

The bar and the big number are the speed flown now. In cruise the small line is the gear you chose, 1 to 26: "gear 5" zooming in, "reverse 3" zooming out, with "boost" while depth speeds the zoom-out up (more in the higher gears). In the autopilot and on a journey, "target" shows your set speed while the speed flown differs from it, for example while speeding up, braking or waiting for the picture. The lane icon is lane assist (**L**): in cruise it steers you away from featureless areas. Steering by hand (**W A S D**, drag, stick) or zooming with the wheel or trackpad takes over. In the autopilot the mouse only suggests a direction: it picks its next places on that side, shown by the target marker's ring.

On iPhone and iPad: tap an icon to switch. The throttle at the edge of the screen sets the gear: drag up for faster in, down for slower and then out, one gear per notch; a double tap on it stops. In cruise and on a journey a drag moves the picture while the zoom goes on (on a journey it takes the wheel); in the autopilot a finger suggests a direction while it is down. The long-press menu is there when nothing flies.

## iterations

How many steps each point gets before Almondbread stops and calls it undecided for now.

**Automatic** raises the limit by itself as you zoom, up to the **upper limit**. **Manual** keeps the number you set. Foggy points are not decided yet: wait, or raise the limit. A blue line at the top shows long renders.

## look

A look sets palette, lighting, outline and the inside's colour in one go. The palette turns escape counts into bands of colour.

Fine-tune the colours below; depth tint shifts them as you zoom.

## interior

Black means proven inside the set. Interior colourings show hidden patterns there: bubbles, how long each repeating loop is, or stripes along the bulbs.

Switching between them is instant.

## overlays

Extras drawn on the picture: the orbit of the point under the pointer (**O**), mini-maps (**M**), the cosmic size scale (**C**) and the π meter (**P**).

Each panel has its own **?** in the corner.

## controls

Point where you want to go and hold **Space** to zoom in (**Shift + Space** zooms out). Drag to move, **R** to start over.

**E** / **Q** cruise in / out (more presses: faster; **Q** home from any depth), **Z** flies on its own, **J** goes on a journey, **N** seeks a minibrot, **L** is lane assist, **F** is full screen, **Esc** stops.
