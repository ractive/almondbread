# Tips

- **Lost?** Press **R** for the whole set, or open the mini-map (**M**) to see where you are.
- **Grey patches?** Those points are not decided yet. Wait a moment, or raise the upper limit in **Iterations & quality**.
- **The best detail lives on the edge.** Zoom where the colours meet the black. Inside the black, and far out in plain colour, nothing new appears.
- **Try a look.** **Look & colour** in the control center changes palette, lighting and the inside in one click.
- **Sit back.** Press **J** for a journey, or **Z** and steer with the mouse.
- **Share a view.** **⇧⌘C** copies its address; anyone can paste it with **⇧⌘V**.
- **Full screen.** Press **F** for a clean picture; **Esc** leaves.
- **Slow deep views?** Less anti-aliasing (**Iterations & quality**) or a smaller window makes them quicker.
